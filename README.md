## 🏗️ 3-Phase Structured Curriculum (All 90 Days Pre-Populated)

Every single day of the 90 days contains **zero placeholders** and delivers **6 timed execution modules** (125–135 minutes total/day):
1. **Module 1: Core Learn (45m)** — In-depth petroleum physics, field insights, and core equations.
2. **Module 2: Watch or Read (20m)** — Curated textbook chapters (Craft & Hawkins, Tiab, Lyons, Dake) and SPE standards.
3. **Module 3: Practice Quiz (30m)** — 10 rigorous technical questions with immediate feedback and automatic error logging.
4. **Module 4: Calculation Problem (15m)** — Whiteboard-style engineering problem with step-by-step mathematical substitution.
5. **Module 5: Interview Drill (15m)** — Real-world interview question with recruiter rationale and model technical answer.
6. **Module 6: Career Action (20m)** — Specific daily deliverable (resume updates, LinkedIn networking, project GitHub commits).
7. **5-Item Daily Checklist & 5-Star Confidence Rating** with notes scratchpad.

### Phase 1: Days 1–30 — "Rebuild the Foundation"
- **Week 1 (Days 1–7): Reservoir Rock & Fluid Fundamentals**
  - Upstream value chain, porosity/permeability, capillary pressure & wettability, phase behavior, Black Oil PVT ($B_o$, $R_s$, $\mu_o$, $c_o$), and real-gas law ($z$-factor).
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14): Reservoir Flow & Material Balance**
  - Steady-state radial Darcy flow, skin factor ($s$), Havlena-Odeh Material Balance (MBE), drive mechanism diagnostic plots, and recovery factor benchmarks.
- **Week 3 (Days 15–21): Drilling Operations & Primary Well Control**
  - Rotary rig components, drilling fluid rheology, Bingham plastic/power law hydraulics, hydrostatic pressure, kick detection signs, Kill Mud Weight (KMW), and Driller's vs. Wait & Weight methods.
- **Week 4 (Days 22–30): Well Completions, Wireline Logging & Artificial Lift**
  - Casing design, cement evaluation (CBL/VDL), open-hole wireline tools (GR, SP, Resistivity, RHOB, NPHI, DT), Archie water saturation ($S_w$), Vogel IPR, and Artificial Lift selection (ESP vs. Gas Lift vs. Sucker Rod Pump).

### Phase 2: Days 31–70 — "Practical Skills & Modern Analytics"
- **Week 5 (Days 31–37): Advanced Formation Evaluation & Shaly Sands**
  - Clay volume ($V_{sh}$) via Larionov, Density-Neutron gas/oil crossover, shaly-sand saturation (Simandoux & Indonesian models), mud logging gas chromatography, and Net Pay cutoffs.
- **Week 6 (Days 38–44): Well Testing & Pressure Transient Analysis (PTA)**
  - Radial diffusivity equation, drawdown testing, semi-log Horner buildup analysis, permeability-thickness ($kh$), skin pressure drop ($\Delta P_{skin}$), and Bourdet pressure derivative log-log diagnostic plots.
- **Week 7 (Days 45–51): Production Surveillance & Inflow Performance**
  - Multi-phase flow regimes, liquid loading in gas wells (Turner criterion), Nodal Analysis system optimization, and Chan Water-Oil Ratio (WOR) log-log diagnostic plots for channeling vs. coning.
- **Week 8 (Days 52–70): Waterflooding, EOR & Subsurface Python Analytics**
  - Buckley-Leverett fractional flow, displacement efficiency, thermal/chemical/gas EOR, Python for petroleum (`numpy`, `pandas`, `matplotlib`, `scipy.optimize`, `lasio`), and automated Decline Curve Analysis (DCA).

### Phase 3: Days 71–90 — "Proof of Ability, Placement Weapons & Job Hunt"
- **Days 71–75: Project 1 — 15-Well Mature Field Production Optimization & Arps DCA**
  - Historical production data cleaning, automated Arps decline fitting, Chan WOR diagnostics, and a $430k 4-well workover campaign delivering +860 BOPD and a 9.5-day payback ($15.1M Year 1 Net Cash).
- **Days 76–80: Project 2 — Equinor Volve Field Digital Petrophysics & Volumetric OOIP**
  - Open-source North Sea LAS log parsing, temperature-corrected $R_w$, Archie saturation calibration in the water leg, 78 ft Net Pay delineation, and 10.85 MMSTB OOIP ($171M asset value).
- **Days 81–86: Placement Weapons & Active Outreach Campaign**
  - Tailoring Resume A (Core Operations) vs. Resume B (Subsurface Analytics), LinkedIn profile algorithm optimization, cold outreach scripts for MIT Pune alumni and recruiters, and 6–8 applications daily.
- **Days 87–89: Interview Bootcamp Simulation**
  - Round 1 Subsurface whiteboard defense, Round 2 Operational drilling/well control defense, and Round 3 Upstream STAR behavioral excellence & salary negotiation.
- **Day 90: Capstone Final Assessment & The Next 30 Days Action Plan**
  - 15-question comprehensive multi-disciplinary exam (85% passing cutoff), printable Certificate of Professional Mastery, and post-bootcamp daily execution protocol.

## ⚡ Interactive Engineering Workbenches

1. **Mission Control Dashboard**: 6 high-level KPI cards, Today's mission briefing, Phase progress milestones, and immediate revision alerts.
2. **Interactive Arps Decline Curve Simulator**: Live sliders for $q_i$, $D_i$, $b$-exponent, and economic limit with real-time SVG rate-time forecast curves and EUR recalculation.
3. **15-Well Surveillance Candidate Matrix**: Diagnostic screening table for bad actors, coning vs. channeling classification, and dynamic workover payback economics.
4. **Interactive 4-Track Composite Well Log Viewer**: Depth-aligned wireline evaluation (Depth, GR/Caliper, Resistivity, Density-Neutron Porosity, and Net Pay/Water saturation flags).
5. **Archie Petrophysical & Volumetric OOIP Solver**: Interactive slider engine tuning cementation ($m$), saturation ($n$), $R_w$, and pattern area with live mathematical substitutions.
6. **Automated Spaced Repetition Revision Queue**: Missed quiz questions are automatically routed to a dedicated revision hub with topic filtering, explanation reveals, and one-click mastery tracking.
7. **Job Application & Outreach Pipeline**: Kanban board and sortable table manager tracking applications across Domestic Operators (Cairn, ONGC, Reliance), Tech Hubs (SLB, Baker Hughes), and Middle East Brownfields.
8. **Dual-Track Resume Studio**: Side-by-side Resume A vs. Resume B copyable markdown, LinkedIn profile transformation copy, and high-conversion cold outreach templates.
9. **Petroleum Calculators**: 4 field-grade engineering solvers (Darcy Radial Flow & Skin, Well Control Kill Mud Weight, Archie Water Saturation, and Vogel Two-Phase IPR).
10. **Interview Bootcamp Flashcard Engine**: Speaking practice timer, recruiter psychology rationale, model technical answers, and must-mention keyword checklists.
11. **Day 90 Capstone Assessment & Certificate**: 15-question exam with instant scoring and an official, printable Certificate of Petroleum Engineering Mastery.

- **Framework**: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (Command-Center Dark Palette: Slate-950/900 background, Crude Amber `#f59e0b`, Offshore Teal `#14b8a6`, Rose `#f43f5e`)
